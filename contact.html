Before you write

Quick answers

A recipe did not work. Can you help?

Yes. Leave a comment on the recipe and tell me what happened, with your oven type if it is a bake. I answer comments faster than email, and your question helps the next cook too.

Can I share your recipes on my own site?

You are welcome to share one photo with a link back to the recipe. Please do not copy the full recipe text or the method.

Do you take sponsored posts?

A few each year, and only for products I already use. Choose "Working together" in the form and tell me a little about the brand.

Where do I find my shop downloads?

Your download link arrives by email right after you pay. If it has not shown up within ten minutes, check your spam folder, then write to me here.
