Cobalt blue, tangerine and butter yellow, arched photos, wavy edges and little spinning badges make your recipes impossible to scroll past. Your readers get a recipe card they can scale and print, and an index that makes every dish easy to find. You install it, press one button, and the whole demo site appears. No code. It is a child theme for Kadence, a free WordPress theme. Kadence does the heavy lifting. Blue Plate adds the design. DESIGN FEATURES • A cobalt logo band with your blog name in a chunky retro script, and a spinning "new recipes every Sunday" badge • A homepage built from nine sections: featured recipes row, category circles, a hello from you, latest recipes with filters, video feature, reader favourites, newsletter sign-up, a row from your shop and an Instagram row • 14 ready-made sections in total. Add, move, remove or repeat any of them on any page • Arched recipe photos, sticker labels, wavy section edges and playful hover effects • Change colors and fonts in the WordPress Customizer • Looks right on phones, tablets and desktops • Fonts are stored inside the theme, so your site does not load fonts from other websites RECIPE FEATURES • A recipe card with scale buttons (half, double, triple), tick boxes for ingredients and a print button • Prep time, cook time, servings and calories at a glance • A recipe index with filter buttons for your categories and a search box just for recipes • Cooking time on a little sticker on every recipe photo • The recipe card runs on the free WP Recipe Maker plugin, which also adds the recipe details Google reads SHOP FEATURES (OPTIONAL) • A shop page with category buttons, and product pages that match your blog • A "From the shop" row for your homepage • Made for ebooks, meal plans and printables • Runs on the free WooCommerce plugin.
