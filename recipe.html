Blueberry Banana Pancakes

Tall, soft and a little bit caramelised at the edges. One bowl, six everyday ingredients, and breakfast is on the table in 20 minutes.

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Serves4
  • Rating★★★★★ 4.9 (128)
Jump to recipe

by Mia · updated 12 November 2026

I have made a lot of flat pancakes in my life. Rubbery ones, pale ones, ones that soaked up half a bottle of syrup and still tasted like nothing. These are the ones I landed on after a whole winter of Saturday mornings, and they have not changed since.

The banana does two jobs here. It sweetens the batter so you need very little sugar, and it keeps the middle soft even if you cook them a minute too long. The blueberries go on top in the pan, not into the bowl, so the batter stays golden and not grey.

Why you will make these again

  • One bowl and a jug. No mixer, no separating eggs.
  • Normal ingredients. Flour, milk, an egg, a banana that has seen better days.
  • They freeze well. Make a double batch and toast them on a school morning.

A few notes on ingredients

Banana. The spottier the better. A yellow banana works, but you will want an extra spoon of sugar.

Baking powder. Check the date on the tub. Old baking powder is the number one reason pancakes stay flat.

Blueberries. Fresh or frozen both work. If they are frozen, do not thaw them first.

Mia's tip

Stop stirring while the batter still looks lumpy. Then leave it alone for five minutes. That short rest is what makes them rise.

How to keep them warm

Put the cooked pancakes on a tray in a low oven, around 100°C, in a single layer. They hold for 20 minutes without drying out, so everyone can sit down at the same time.

Blueberry Banana Pancakes

Fluffy one bowl pancakes sweetened with ripe banana and finished with blueberries and maple syrup.

★★★★★ 4.9 from 128 ratings

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook10 min
  • Total20 min
  • Makes8 pancakes
Scale

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mix the dry things. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.

  2. Mix the wet things. In a jug, mash the banana until smooth. Whisk in the egg, milk and melted butter.

  3. Bring them together. Pour the wet mix into the bowl and stir until just combined. Lumps are good. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.

  4. Cook. Heat a non stick pan over medium heat with a little butter. Pour in about 60 ml of batter for each pancake and scatter a few blueberries on top.

  5. Flip. After about 2 minutes, when bubbles show and the edges look set, flip and cook for 1 to 2 minutes more.

  6. Serve. Stack them up and finish with banana, the rest of the blueberries and maple syrup.

Notes

To freeze: cool completely, layer with baking paper and freeze for up to one month. Reheat in the toaster.

No banana? Use 60 g of plain yoghurt and one more tablespoon of sugar.
