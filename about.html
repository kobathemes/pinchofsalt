hello, I'm Mia

I write recipes for people who are tired by six o'clock.

Pinch of Salt started as a notebook on my fridge. I wrote down the dinners that worked and crossed out the ones that did not. Friends started asking for the notebook, so I put it online.

Today it is a small corner of the internet with more than a hundred recipes, all tested in a normal kitchen with a normal oven that runs a little hot.

What you will find here

Three promises I keep

  1. Honest timings

    If it says 30 minutes, it means 30 minutes for a normal person, chopping included.

  2. Normal ingredients

    Everything comes from an ordinary supermarket. If something is unusual, I give you a swap.

  3. Tested three times

    Once by me, once on a busy weeknight, and once by a friend who does not cook much.

The kitchen

Small, a bit messy, and always in use

I cook on four burners and one oven shelf. There is no studio and no team. The photos are taken by the window before the food goes cold, and then we eat it.

That matters, because it means every recipe has been made the way you will make it. On a weeknight, with one eye on the clock.
